# 2026-10-22

## The app now installs on the owner's devices

The iPhone runs iOS 18.7.8 and the iPad iOS 18.5, so the deployment target dropped from iOS 26 to 18.
Liquid Glass on the toast and the shrinking tab bar stay, behind `#available(iOS 26, *)`.
`npm run ios` now skips simulators and counts an install on a locked device as installed.

## Simpler and always light (`a6c1962`)

Feedback: "UI UX and design not simple enough, and also the color for our app shouldn't be dark."

- The dark teal (`#1B6E66`) became a bright green (`#1DA35A`), chosen for Tracy's *Eat That Frog*. Green is only for ticks, the A letter and the one primary button; red only for problems.
- The app no longer follows dark mode (`UIUserInterfaceStyle` Light).
- Screen titles moved to New York serif to match Today's date header.
- Goals went from seven sections to one list: main goal, A1 card, the rest with letters on the right, Needs a letter with a Letter menu, then Plan tomorrow and Bring in a schedule. The Today/Tomorrow switch, the goal list and the 21 dots moved one tap deeper.
- Study lost the calendar status, the budget line and the due dots, and only says a status when it needs attention.
- The Add sheet's letter picker now shows plainly which letter is chosen.

Pitfall found: setting `UINavigationBar.appearance()` in `App.init` silently replaced the green with system blue. It now happens in `AppDelegate`.

## Microsoft To Do's task details on every tab (`016ef8b`)

Request: "All tabs must have the features of Microsoft To Do, set deadline time date, notify time date, and so on."
A Haiku research agent inventoried Microsoft To Do from Microsoft's support pages; the menu wording follows it ("Remind me", "Later today", "Pick a date and time", "Add due date", "Repeat", "Add step").

- Core: `Todo` gained a due date and time, `remindAt`, `recur`, steps, a note, a star and `createdAt`; `Item` gained `remindAt`, steps and `myDay`; `Recur` gained `yearly` and `interval`. All optional, decoded leniently, so older saves and the website's backups still load.
- Ticking off a repeating to-do adds the next one (never in the past, reminder moved along, steps unticked); ticking it back on removes that untouched next one.
- Reminders are local notifications with Mark done and Remind me in 10 minutes; tapping one opens the task. Permission is asked only when a reminder is first set.
- Today: typed dates are read, five sorts, completed section, items added to Today.
- Search across tabs, list settings in More, a generated completion chime (`ios/WilList/done.wav`), an app icon badge, home-screen quick actions.

Pitfall found: on the simulator, an unanswered permission prompt from an earlier build kept reappearing after reinstalling; `xcrun simctl erase` cleared it.
