## Colour: the poster, course colours, Focus and the week chart

The owner asked to "make it a visually captivating experience", then said there are no rules, for anything, including the single green.
The old notes ("always light, one bright green, never add another hue") were treated as the owner's choices of the time, not limits, and `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/DESIGN.md` now say so.

- **The A1 poster** (`e54c83c`): in Meadow, the day's A1 is no longer a pale card but a field of colour across the top of Today, running up under the status bar, its title 44 pt bold serif in white.
  Mark done draws a line through the title left to right, the words fade, and the next A rises into the same place.
  Recordings found three flaws on the way, each fixed before committing:
  - The old and new titles crossed for a frame, because the list animates the row's height while both are on screen; the old words now fade out first.
  - The next A arrived already crossed out for a moment, because "done" belonged to the card rather than to the row; it is kept per row now.
  - On the first A of a streak, the new "1 day in a row" line landed on the Start button while the buttons were still moving up; it now arrives once the card has settled.
- **Course colours** (`eee2210`): the chosen signature, like a highlighter per class.
  Each course keeps one of eight colours that hold white text at 4.5:1 or better (blue, tangerine, plum, teal, raspberry, ochre, slate, then the green, which already means "no course").
  The plan stores the assignment as `settings.courseColors` (course key to palette number), given by `AppState.colorCourses` on every change and on loading; a new course takes the least used colour and a course nobody names any more gives its colour back.
  It is an optional field, decoded leniently, and the website keeps unknown settings, so older saves still load and the website round-trips it.
  The poster, the A1 widget and the Focus screen take the A1's course colour, and course names on rows are written in theirs.
  Focus became a full-screen field with a white ring and timer.
  A recording showed the poster staying blue when the next A belonged to another course: the list doesn't repaint a row's list background when only its colour changes, so the field is now painted inside the rows.
- **The week chart:** Study's bars now stack each day's study by course colour, with a key under the chart, so a heavy day shows which course makes it heavy.
  Red stacked on tangerine read badly, so a day past the budget now shows it by crossing the dashed line and by its time in red under the bar.

Signing for devices is blocked until the owner accepts Apple's latest Program License Agreement ("PLA Update available"); the release build was compiled with `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO` and has no warnings.

## A clean hand-over between courses

Recorded at 60 fps, Mark done between two courses (Problem set 5 in blue, then Email TA in tangerine) had four flaws.
The field blended through a grey mauve for a third of a second, "Mark done" and "Done" overlapped while one faded into the other, the new title was drawn with the old one's strike line reshaping into it, and the old Done check lingered where the button used to be.
The first cause was the toast host: `.animation(.snappy, value: store.toast)` sat on the whole screen, so the Undo toast, shown in the same update, animated Today's list and the list crossfaded the poster.
That animation now stays on the toast.
The field is a `RisingField`: the new course's colour wipes up from the bottom over the old one in half a second, under the status bar too, and its colours never animate themselves.
The card brings the next A's words in itself, rising 28 points as they fade in; the struck copy of the title and the button's label are keyed by the row, so the next A starts fresh, and the button's words change at once while only its check morphs.
A taller or shorter next title still makes the card change height.
Several ways to make that glide were tried and recorded: a held height animated inside the card, the same height kept by Today, deferring it a moment, and animating the whole change.
A `List` only animates a row's height as part of an animated change to its data, and that animates everything in the row: the old title came back over the new one and the blue went orange through a vivid blue.
So the card takes its new height in one step while it is empty, between the old words fading and the new ones rising, which reads as the card being set for the next thing.
Undo now does the same when it brings back another A1 (`Store.undoChangesA1`); other undos still animate.
The three List lessons are pitfalls 22 to 24 in DESIGN.

## Mark done on a reminder crashed the app

Pressing Mark done on a reminder, with WilList running in the background, stopped the app instead of ticking the to-do.
The reminder handler was an async method off the main actor, so it finished on another thread, and UIKit stops an app when the completion it calls then runs anywhere but the main thread.
The handler now runs on the main actor from start to end, and the to-do is done the moment the button is pressed.
A new UI test, `testMarkDoneFromAReminder`, starts from a plan whose to-do has a reminder within 75 seconds, goes to the Home Screen, waits for the reminder, presses its Mark done and checks the to-do is done; it failed with "com.lull.willist crashed" before the fix and passes after.
It is the slowest test (up to 80 seconds, waiting for the minute), and the only way to reach this code without a device.
