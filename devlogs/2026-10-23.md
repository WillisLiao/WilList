# 2026-10-23

## Colour: the poster, course colours, Focus and the week chart

The owner asked to "make it a visually captivating experience", then said there are no rules, for anything, including the single green.
The old notes ("always light, one bright green, never add another hue") were treated as the owner's choices of the time, not limits, and `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/DESIGN.md` now say so.

- **The A1 poster** (`e54c83c`): in Meadow, the day's A1 is no longer a pale card but a field of colour across the top of Today, running up under the status bar, its title 44 pt bold serif in white.
  Mark done draws a line through the title left to right, the words fade, and the next A rises into the same place.
  Recordings found three flaws on the way, each fixed before committing:
  - The old and new titles crossed for a frame, because the list animates the row's height while both are on screen; the old words now fade out first.
  - The next A arrived already crossed out for a moment, because "done" belonged to the card rather than to the row; it is kept per row now.
  - On the first A of a streak, the new "1 day in a row" line landed on the Start button while the buttons were still moving up; it now arrives once the card has settled.
- **Course colours** (`eee2210`): the chosen signature, like a highlighter per class.
  Each course keeps one of eight colours that hold white text at 4.5:1 or better (blue, tangerine, plum, teal, raspberry, ochre, slate, then the green, which already means "no course").
  The plan stores the assignment as `settings.courseColors` (course key to palette number), given by `AppState.colorCourses` on every change and on loading; a new course takes the least used colour and a course nobody names any more gives its colour back.
  It is an optional field, decoded leniently, and the website keeps unknown settings, so older saves still load and the website round-trips it.
  The poster, the A1 widget and the Focus screen take the A1's course colour, and course names on rows are written in theirs.
  Focus became a full-screen field with a white ring and timer.
  A recording showed the poster staying blue when the next A belonged to another course: the list doesn't repaint a row's list background when only its colour changes, so the field is now painted inside the rows.
- **The week chart:** Study's bars now stack each day's study by course colour, with a key under the chart, so a heavy day shows which course makes it heavy.
  Red stacked on tangerine read badly, so a day past the budget now shows it by crossing the dashed line and by its time in red under the bar.

Signing for devices is blocked until the owner accepts Apple's latest Program License Agreement ("PLA Update available"); the release build was compiled with `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO` and has no warnings.

## A dropped tap on CI

CI failed once on `b617c96`: `testMarkTheA1Done` waited for Undo after Mark done, and Undo never came.
The kept results showed why: the tap was synthesized dead centre on the button, the screen recording shows no press and no change for the next ten seconds, and nothing covered the app.
The simulator dropped the tap; the app never saw it.
The test now taps through a helper that waits for what the tap brings up and tries once more if, after five seconds, nothing has.

## The guided flows on a field

The welcome and Plan tomorrow were plain white pages while Today now speaks in fields of colour with big white serif type.
Each step's question now sits in white on a field across the top: green for the welcome, night indigo for Plan tomorrow, because it is an evening ritual.
Plan tomorrow's last page, "Tomorrow starts with", fills with the colour of tomorrow's A1 course, previewing the poster the student will see in the morning.
On the way, the small lines on fields were dimmed white (80 to 85%), which falls below 4.5:1 on the green; every word on a field is pure white now, on Today's poster, Focus and the widget too.

## "Your A’s are done."

Marking the day's last A done used to collapse the poster back into a plain header: the biggest win of the day ended in an anticlimax.
Now the poster stays, green (the colour of everything done), and says "Your A’s are done." with a check, how many things got done today and the streak, which is Tracy's jolt of energy for finishing made visible.
Recordings of the hand-over found two flaws, both fixed:
- The list crossfaded two separate rows over white, so the field washed pale for two frames; the A1 and the new poster are now one row whose contents swap.
- The old buttons ghosted over the new words. Fading the whole card first didn't take at first: the departing card re-read the plan, found no rows left and turned visible again. "This is the last A" is now decided when Mark done is tapped and kept in the card.
- Another CI failure (`34fd307`): Search's X didn't close Search.
  The kept results showed the tap point was the X's position while the bar was still sliding it into place after the to-do's details closed, not where it ended up.
  The test now waits for the details to be gone, and the dropped-tap helper (now taking any check) gives the X one more try.
