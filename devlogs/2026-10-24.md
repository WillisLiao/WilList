# 2026-10-24

## A tab bar that stays white

The owner said the white tab bar "keeps changing to background color when pressed or scrolled".
Sampling the screenshots' pixels confirmed it: iOS 26's floating glass tab bar takes on the colour of whatever is under it, so over the slate it turned slate-blue, and it shrank on scroll.
Neither `toolbarBackground(.white, for: .tabBar)` nor a `UITabBarAppearance` with a white background changed a single pixel there.
So WilList draws its own: `TabBar` in `WilListApp.swift`, the same four tabs on a solid white capsule with a soft shadow, the chosen one in the deep teal on a pale teal pill, a selection haptic, and an identifier and the selected trait on each button for tests and VoiceOver.
It sits over the `TabView`, and each tab hides the system's with `underTabBar()`.
The first try left its 76 pt of room with a `safeAreaInset` on each tab, outside the navigation stack, where it reached nothing: `testMarkTheA1Done` failed because the Undo toast sat behind the bar and the tap on Undo landed on More, and Plan's last row couldn't scroll out from under it.
Each screen's toast host, inside its stack, now leaves the room (passed down as `tabBarRoom`), so the toast sits above the bar and every list scrolls clear.
The keyboard covers it rather than lifting it, and full-screen pages (Focus, Plan tomorrow, the welcome) cover it as they covered the system's.
On the iPad the system's tabs at the top are hidden too, and the same capsule sits at the bottom.
The UI test that tapped `app.tabBars.buttons["Today"]` now taps `tab-today`.

## As few words as do the job

The owner: "the app has too many words, each item, thing, description should have minimal words as possible".
Every visible string in the app and widgets was cut.
All explanatory footers went (More lost five, the editors three), except where a footer changes what you do next ("50 min short. Raise daily study in More.").
Labels lost what the screen already says: "Starred on top", "Read dates in titles", "Per day: 2 h", "Back up", "Restore".
Statuses became fragments: "3-day streak", "2 need a letter", "2 sessions behind", "Missed yesterday", "Dashed line: 2 h a day".
Empty cards keep a short bold title and one button: Study's paragraph became "No more cramming".
Tracy's ideas keep their homes in fewer words: the letters say "Serious consequences", "Mild consequences", "No consequences", "Hand it off", "Drop it"; the victim-language nudges say "Don’t try. Decide." and "Call it a situation."; the guided flows ask their questions with one short line under each ("If you could do only one, that’s your A1.").
The rules are in `docs/DESIGN.md`, Words, and pitfall 27 records the tab bar.

## Waiting for CI, properly this time

Yesterday's note said waits for CI now name their commit, but they still hung: `gh run list --commit` matches only a full 40-character SHA, and with a short one it prints nothing, which a wait loop reads as "not finished yet".
CI on `0081f87` had passed in 19 minutes while the wait kept spinning.
Waits now pass `$(git rev-parse <sha>)` and should carry a timeout of their own.

## A new A comes last

The owner asked why they couldn't choose A1, A2 and A3, then said it had worked correctly and spelled out the rule they expect: an A added later takes the next number, and dragging it up in Plan ranks it higher.
Dragging works and stays as it is.
The second half didn't always hold: a new letter was placed by its due date, and an undated one by a clock value that wrapped every 17 minutes, so a new A could land above A's added earlier.
A new letter now lands after every item that already has it (`defaultOrder(_:after:)`), and picking the same letter again no longer reshuffles an item that had been dragged into place.
Both have core tests.

## Everything planned stays in Plan

The owner set a date and time on "medic ad read" and it left Plan for Study: "I should be able to see all plans in plans, and only todays in today, and whichever of those two include study then they will be in study tab".
Plan listed only the day's rows, so anything whose study starts on a later day, an exam weeks away or a repeat on another day, was in no list but Study's.
Plan now ends with **Later**: every lettered item that isn't on today's list, soonest first, with its letter but no number (only the day's list is numbered, and the A1 stays the day's first thing).
Today is unchanged: today's A1, the to-dos, and what was added to Today.

The owner also asked for the date and time to be picked in one row.
The due row is now the calendar, the date and the time side by side; "+ Time" sits where the time goes until one is set, and the clear button takes off the time first, then the date.
The word "Due" beside both squeezed onto two lines, so the row is just the calendar and the two chips, and VoiceOver still reads "Due".
