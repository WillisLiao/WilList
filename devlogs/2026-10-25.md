## No more kinds: every item is a task

The owner, asked whether the Agent and calendar links should keep setting exams, deadlines and readings: "we dont need to distinguish exam, deadline, presentation, reading, class or task as a whole, there's ur answer, listing them is enough, organizing them based on those kinds are useless".
So WilList no longer tells items apart by kind, anywhere.

**What went:**
- The kinds themselves. `ItemType` is now `task` or `study` (a study block the student sets); any other kind in an older save loads as a task, so nothing in a save is lost.
- The study planner built on them: `Plan.swift` and `Timeline.swift` (prep sessions spread back from an exam, start dates, shortfalls, calibrating your pace, replanning), with their tests. An item no longer carries sessions, a grade weight, an effort estimate or a start date, and Settings lost session length, buffer days, day start and day end.
- The Agent's `propose_items` asks only for title, date, time, course and the days a weekly thing repeats on. The prompt tells it every item is a plain task. Its `read_plan` marks study blocks and nothing else.
- Calendar links make every entry a task (`detectType` is gone).
- Progress's Coming up (each exam and deadline and whether it was on track), and the suggestions to replan missed sessions or adjust your pace. Progress keeps the last 7 days, the suggestions to start an A early or review old C to E items, and the next 7 days' study blocks.
- The suggested letter in the letter sorter and the letter list (it came from the kind and the grade share).
- The study plan section in an item's details; the sheet is titled Task or Study block.

**What stays:** letters and the A1, dates, times, reminders, repeats, steps, notes, courses and their colours, study blocks and the daily study time they are measured against, focus with real minutes, and the done chart.
The save format only lost fields the app writes; the decoder still reads every older save (`olderKindsLoadAsTasks`).

The Agent's demo script (`-agentDemo YES`) proposes three plain tasks with the new fields.
`scripts/ios-shots.mjs` lost `-scroll coming`.
`docs/PRINCIPLES.md` says plainly that sub-deadlines and spaced repetition are not in the app, at the owner's word; `docs/DESIGN.md` and the README match the app.

The diff is about 1,000 lines lighter, most of it in the core.
