## Numeric dates, details, an Agent that is not a chat

- Dates beyond the week read as month and day numbers, "11/9" (`Fmt.numeric`, also the date picker's quick picks, the date button and the study block summary); weekdays stay "Mon".
- In a task's details, Add to Today applies at once (`ItemEditor.addedToToday` writes the stored item too); everything else waits for Save. The letter picker is the same row of five coloured chips as in New task, in its own section.

## The sketch hand

At the owner's ask ("for the countdown of play button, focus countdown, it could be more sketchy ... like creative sketch, not too many, not too little, just simple, and adds a twist"), clarified as "it's just not specific for countdown, it's specific for the whole app".

The idea came from the method rather than from a style: WilList is Tracy's written list, where you letter each task with a pen and circle the one that comes first.
So the marks that say "this one" are now drawn rather than set.
The twist is that one pen circle carries that meaning everywhere: the check you have ticked, the focus ring you are inside, the tab you are in, and the A1.

`ios/WilList/Sketch.swift` holds the whole language in three shapes and one colour: `SketchCircle` (ten hand-set points run through a smooth curve, starting at the top and going clockwise so a trim from 0 shows time; an overshoot carries the pen past its own start and crosses it), `SketchTick`, `SketchRule`, and `Color.pencil`.
The wobble is hand-set rather than random, so every mark in the app is the same pen and nothing shimmers when a list is laid out again.

Used in five places and no more:

- Every circle you tick or choose, through one view (`DrawnCheck`): every row's check on every tab, a step in a task's details, Today's add bar once a line is being written, and the guided flows' written lines and "Which one first?" rows.
  Where a circle is only ever empty it is `DrawnCheck(done: false)`, so the app has one circle and not two.
- The focus ring, with no overshoot (a quarter drawn must be a quarter of the time), and the time left moved to the system's rounded face, which sits with a drawn ring where the plain one did not.
- The chosen tab's symbol, circled in the deep teal in place of the pale teal pill.
- The A1's letter on Today and in Plan, circled in graphite.
- A rule under the day's date on Today and Plan.

Three things found by looking at the render rather than the code:

- A filled sketch circle with no stroke reads as a blob, not as a drawn mark; keeping the pen edge when it fills (and the little tail past the top) fixed it.
- A closed circle whose curve reads its neighbours only within the array leaves a sharp peak where the pen meets its start, plain at Break where the ring is full.
  The closed variant now reads its neighbours round the loop (`wrap`).
- The first ring round a tab's symbol was 36 by 30 and cut through the wider symbols; 42 by 32 goes round them.

Motion is unchanged in kind: the tick is trimmed by whether the row is done, so a tap draws it on while a row that is already done, scrolled into view, simply has it.
Nothing draws itself on arrival, and Reduce Motion is respected as before.
Deliberately left alone: the letters' chips, the star, the course colours, the charts, and the widgets (pitfall 13).
The Agent's find rows still tick with the system's circles, as this session was told not to touch the Agent; that is the one place the language stops short.

Checked: 77 core tests, lint, a zero-warning release build, the UI tests, and every changed screen looked at in the simulator (Today, Plan, Progress, Goals, the add bar mid-line, a row the moment it is ticked, Focus part-way and at Break, and Focus at Display Zoom's 320 points).
