# 2026-10-28

## Numeric dates, details, an Agent that is not a chat

- Dates beyond the week read as month and day numbers, "11/9" (`Fmt.numeric`, also the date picker's quick picks, the date button and the study block summary); weekdays stay "Mon".
- In a task's details, Add to Today applies at once (`ItemEditor.addedToToday` writes the stored item too); everything else waits for Save. The letter picker is the same row of five coloured chips as in New task, in its own section.
- The Agent is upload-only, at the owner's word: no text field, no questions, no analysis. Choose a file or a photo, Find tasks, and the finds come as a card to tick and Add. `propose_items` is its only tool: `read_plan` and `follow_calendar` are gone, so the plan is never sent and no student text reaches the model. `Agent.run` is `run(_ messages, today:, route:)`; tests cover that nothing but `propose_items` exists.
